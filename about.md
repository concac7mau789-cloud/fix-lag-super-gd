# Fix Lag Super

Reduce lag and stutter in Geometry Dash.

- **Disable particles** while inside levels
- **Disable shaders** (shader triggers are ignored when a level loads)
- **Force Low Detail Mode** while inside levels (your normal setting is restored afterwards)
- **Clean memory** before a level loads and after you leave it
- **Disable camera shake** triggers

Open the mod's settings in Geode to turn each option on or off. Shader changes apply the next time you enter a level.
